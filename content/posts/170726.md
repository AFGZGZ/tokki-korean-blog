If you are currently learning Korean, open your phone and count how many apps you are using. You probably have one for flashcards, another for grammar reference, a heavy dictionary app, and a browser tab open to a Korean video or web novel.

Juggling four or five tools just to get through a single study session fragments your focus and slows down your progress.

I quietly launched Tokki Korean on the Google Play Store with a simple mission: to build the ultimate, zero-friction ecosystem for Korean language learners.

## The All-in-One Language Toolkit

Tokki Korean brings together the essential tools for language mastery into a single, seamless environment. Whether you are learning your first set of vocabulary or prepping for advanced proficiency exams, you no longer have to jump between apps.

Here is what is packed into Tokki Korean:

Flashcards & SRS: Build and retain your vocabulary using spaced repetition system (SRS) learning, complete with audio pronunciations and contextual example sentences.

Contextual Reading Support: Import your own PDF, EPUB, or TXT ebooks. If you hit a word you don't know, simply tap it to immediately reveal its meaning, grammatical particles, and structure without leaving the page.

Interactive Video Learning: Watch YouTube videos inside the app with interactive Korean subtitles. Tap unfamiliar words mid-video to instantly view their definitions and save them straight to your vocabulary deck.

Integrated Premium Dictionary: Skip the clunky external searches. Get comprehensive Korean definitions, usage examples, and essential grammar point disambiguation natively.

Gamified Reinforcement: Test your skills with targeted TOPIK practice tests and interactive games designed to build long-term memory.

## A Language App That Respects You

Beyond the features, I built Tokki Korean on a foundation that many modern apps ignore: privacy and accessibility.

Tokki Korean requires no account creation and collects zero user data. Even better? The app works offline, meaning you can study during your morning subway commute, or anywhere else without worrying about cellular data.
